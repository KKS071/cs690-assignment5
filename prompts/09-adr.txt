Question to the main agent (verbatim, HANDOUT.md Part 9):

Look at how app/service.py and app/repository.py check for an overlap and then
insert a booking. Can two requests that arrive at the same moment both pass the
check? List the ways to prevent it with SQLite, with one advantage and one cost
each. Do not edit files.

Answer, in short: yes. has_overlap, names_on_date and insert_booking each open their
own connection and transaction, so two threads can both read "no overlap" before
either inserts. A script with 8 threads in 30 trials (evidence/race_check.py) saved
more than one overlapping booking in 28/30 trials. The options listed were: a BEGIN
IMMEDIATE write transaction around check+insert; a process-wide threading.Lock; a
SQLite trigger that raises on overlap; an INSERT ... SELECT ... WHERE NOT EXISTS
single statement; and doing nothing. Each came with one advantage and one cost (see
docs/adr/0001-serialize-booking-writes.md).
